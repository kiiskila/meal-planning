Meal prep for two

Twelve interchangeable week-long options, grouped by season. Every shared dish is vegetarian, with Japanese, Korean and Polish cooking at the centre; chicken or kotlety are cooked separately for one of us. Each option has a PDF guide with the week at a glance, the shopping list, and how to cook and split every dish.

Polish Vegetarian Year round Lunch

Barszcz czerwony (Polish red borscht)

Polonist · rated 4.8 (23 ratings) · makes 4
369kcal / serving
8 gprotein
105 mintotal
65 gcarbs
In this plan
Make 1 1/4 x the recipe (5 servings). Per serving the recipe page also lists 9 g fat and 17 g fibre.
Storage
Up to 7 days in the fridge in a lidded container (author).
Reheat
Reheat gently, 2 min in the microwave; do not boil it hard or the colour dulls.
Allergens
None of the major allergens (sulphites if you use the wine)
My notes
Polish barszcz czerwony, the clear beet broth, not the chunky Ukrainian soup. It needs about 500 mL of beet kvass (zakwas), a few dried wild mushrooms and 2 prunes, none of which are costed: a Polish deli sells kvass, or the author links her own 5 day recipe. It is a light soup, so it is paired with bread and something more filling.
